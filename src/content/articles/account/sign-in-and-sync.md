## Sign in or create an account

1. Open **More › Sign In / Create Account**. The app also asks when you do something that needs an account, such as creating a trip.
2. Choose **Sign in with Google**, **Sign in with Apple**, or **Sign in with e-mail**. All three work on iPhone, Android and the web.
3. New here? Choose **Create a new account** and enter your email and a password (at least 6 characters).

Use the **same method every time**. Signing in with Google one day and with an email password the next can create two separate accounts if the addresses differ. You can connect Google and Apple to an existing account in the [Customer Portal](https://customer.tripomatic.com/).

## How syncing works

- Trips, favorites and settings live in your Tripomatic account.
- Sign in with the same account on another phone or at [maps.tripomatic.com](https://maps.tripomatic.com/) and everything is there. There is no sync button; changes appear on their own.
- The apps work offline: edits are kept on the device and uploaded when you reconnect. When you are offline the app shows "No internet connection".
- Premium also follows your account, not the device.

## Sign out

**More › Settings › Account › Sign Out**, then confirm.

## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot Password?**, enter your email and tap **Send Reset Email**. Check spam if it does not arrive. The link opens the Customer Portal, where you set a new password (at least 8 characters).
