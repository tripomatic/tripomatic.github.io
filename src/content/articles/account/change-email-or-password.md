## Change your email or password

1. In the app, open **More › Settings**, expand **Account** and tap **Manage Account**. This opens the [Customer Portal](https://customer.tripomatic.com/) in your browser. You can also go there directly and sign in.
2. Under **Account**, choose **Change E-mail** or **Change Password**.
3. For a new email address, open the verification link we send **to the new address**. The change applies once you confirm it.

The portal is also where you connect or disconnect **Google** and **Apple** sign-in.

## Change your name

1. Open **More › Settings** and expand **Account**.
2. Tap the pencil (**Edit Name**), type your name and tap **Save**.

Your email address is shown there but cannot be edited in the app.
