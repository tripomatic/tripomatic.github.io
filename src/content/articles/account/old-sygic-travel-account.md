Sygic Travel accounts do not work in today's Tripomatic app on their own. You link the old account to a Tripomatic account once, and your data comes across.

## Step 1: Link the accounts

1. Open [customer.tripomatic.com/link-sygic](https://customer.tripomatic.com/link-sygic) in a browser. The page is titled **Bring over your Sygic Travel account**.
2. Enter the **email address of your old Sygic Travel account**, exactly as it was (without accents), and tap **Send code**.
3. Enter the **6-digit code** from that inbox and tap **Verify code**. No email after a few minutes, even in spam? Then no Sygic Travel account uses that address; try another address you may have used.
4. Choose **Create a Tripomatic account** or **Sign in to an existing Tripomatic account**.
5. Check the two addresses shown and tap **Link the accounts**.
6. You see **Accounts linked**.

## Step 2: Open the app

Sign in to Tripomatic (iPhone, Android or [maps.tripomatic.com](https://maps.tripomatic.com/)) with **the same Tripomatic account**. Your trips and favorites are imported automatically, which can take a few minutes. If nothing appears, close and reopen the app.

A **lifetime licence or subscription** bought under Sygic Travel comes across with the link.

## Missing trips later?

Open **More › Settings › Migration Assistant**. It lists your old trips with **Not migrated** and **Migrated** filters and lets you import them again:

- **Migrate my account** imports everything that is missing.
- **Migrate trip** imports one trip.
- **Re-migrate** replaces a trip with a fresh copy from the old account. This **overwrites every change** you made to it in Tripomatic.

## If linking is refused

The page tells you when it cannot link, for example because your Tripomatic account already has a Sygic account linked, or the Sygic account is linked to someone else. Email support@tripomatic.com with both email addresses and the message you saw.
