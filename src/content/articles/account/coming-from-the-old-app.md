Tripomatic 26 launched on **4 February 2026** and replaced the previous apps (the older Tripomatic app and, before it, Sygic Travel). It is one app for iPhone, Android and the web at [maps.tripomatic.com](https://maps.tripomatic.com/), with new accounts.

## Getting your old data

Old accounts are linked once, then trips, favorites and Premium come across: [Bring over your old Sygic Travel account](/account/old-sygic-travel-account/). Sharing between collaborators is not carried over; invite people again.

## What is different

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Offline maps by city or region | [Offline maps per trip and per day](/on-the-road/offline-maps/), removed 30 days after the trip |
| Facebook sign-in | Email, Google or Apple sign-in |
| PDF generated on the phone | [PDF generated on our servers](/sharing-and-export/export-trip/), with maps and photos, sent by email too |
| Suggest edits to places | **Report an issue** on a place |
| Profile picture | No profile picture |
| Paid upfront for lifetime | Free plan with ads, or a yearly [Premium subscription](/premium/free-vs-premium/); old lifetime licences still work |
