## Where the settings are

Open **More › Settings**. You will find:

- **Language**: 27 languages, listed by their own names. It is saved to your account.
- **Currency**: 31 currencies, used for costs and prices in your trips.
- **Distance Units**: **Metric (km, m)** or **Imperial (mi, ft)**.
- **Temperature Units**: **Celsius (°C)** or **Fahrenheit (°F)**.
- **Time Format**: **12-hour (1:30 PM)** or **24-hour (13:30)**.

## Defaults

Until you choose, units follow your device's **region**, not its language: the US uses miles and Fahrenheit, the UK miles and Celsius, most other countries kilometres and Celsius. Time format follows your phone's 12/24-hour setting. Anything you pick yourself overrides the default.

Other settings in the same place: **Marketing Emails**, **Privacy › Manage Privacy Settings**, **Maps and Cache**, **Default navigation app** and **Daily summary & alerts** (phones only).
