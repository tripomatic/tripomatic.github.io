## Before you delete

1. **Cancel your subscription** where you bought it: see [Cancel or manage your subscription](/premium/cancel-subscription/). App Store and Google Play subscriptions keep billing until you cancel them in the store.
2. **Export anything you want to keep.** Premium users can [export trips to PDF, GPX or KML](/sharing-and-export/export-trip/).

## Delete the account

1. Open **More › Settings** and expand **Account**.
2. Tap **Cancel Account** (in red).
3. Read the warning. It shows how many trips will be deleted and whether you have active Premium. Tap **Cancel Account**.
4. Enter the **6-digit verification code** we email to you, and confirm.
5. You see "Your account has been canceled." and are signed out.

You can do the same in the [Customer Portal](https://customer.tripomatic.com/) under **Delete account**.
