## Plan a route to a place

1. Open a place (or a stop in your trip) and tap **Navigate**. You can also open **More › Navigate**.
2. The route starts from **Current location**; tap it to choose another start.
3. Pick how to travel: Walking, Car, Bike, Scooter, Taxi, Public transit and more. For public transport choose **Depart after** or **Arrive by**; for driving you can **Avoid toll roads** or **Avoid ferries**.
4. Compare the alternatives (Best, Fastest, Shortest).

## Turn-by-turn directions (iPhone and Android)

1. In the route, tap **Navigate**.
2. Choose an installed app in **Navigate with**. Tick **Set as default** to skip this question next time.

Change or reset the default app in **More › Settings › Default navigation app**. The navigation app gets the start point you chose, not your live position from Tripomatic.

## Send a route by email

Tap **Send navigation link to email** in the route. Useful when you plan on a computer and drive with your phone.

## Show where you are

Tap the **location** button on the map:

1. once to centre on your location;
2. again to follow you;
3. again to rotate the map with your direction.

If it says **Location permission denied**, allow location for Tripomatic in your phone's settings. On the web, click the padlock in the address bar and allow location.

## See a day on the map

Selecting a day shows its places and routes on the map. Use the day menu **⋮ › Show on map** to zoom to it, or **Show only this day's items** to hide everything else.
