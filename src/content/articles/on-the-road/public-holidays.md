## On your trip

On any dated trip, holidays and special days at that day's places appear as boxes under the day notes. Tap one to see its name, type, dates and **What to expect**, for example closures or road closures.

## On destinations

Destination pages show **Coming up** for the next 7 days, and the guide lists upcoming **Public holidays** and **Special occasions**.

## Opening hours

Opening hours in Tripomatic consider public holidays, so a museum closed on a holiday shows as closed that day.

For a full list by country, see [public holidays on tripomatic.com](https://tripomatic.com/en/features/public-holidays).
