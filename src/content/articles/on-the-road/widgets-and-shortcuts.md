## Today widget

**iPhone:** touch and hold the home screen, tap **Edit › Add Widget**, search for **Tripomatic**, choose **Today** and a size (small, medium, large, or the lock-screen sizes).

**Android:** touch and hold the home screen, tap **Widgets**, find **Tripomatic** and drag **Today** to the screen. It can be resized.

Before a trip it counts down the days; during the trip it shows **Day N of N** with today's plan. With no upcoming trip it offers **Plan your next trip**. Tap it to open the app.

## App shortcuts

Touch and hold the Tripomatic icon:

- **Today**: opens today's plan.
- **Search nearby**: searches around you.
- **Next trip**: opens your current or next trip, shown when you have one.

## Siri (iPhone, iOS 16 or later)

Say, in English:

- "**Open today in Tripomatic**"
- "**What's next in Tripomatic**", which answers aloud without opening the app
- "**Open my Lisbon trip in Tripomatic**" (use your trip's name)
- "**Show my trips in Tripomatic**"

These also appear in the Shortcuts app and Spotlight. You need to be signed in.
