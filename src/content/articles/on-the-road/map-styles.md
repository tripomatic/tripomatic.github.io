1. Tap the **layers** button on the map (tooltip "Map styles").
2. Choose a style:
   - **Streets**: the default, free.
   - **Minimal**: a calmer map, free, with **Auto**, **Light** and **Dark** brightness.
   - **Satellite**, **Outdoor** and **Winter**: Premium. You can preview them before upgrading.

If maps look broken or slow on an older phone, turn on **More › Settings › Compatibility Mode**. It simplifies map drawing but disables offline maps.
