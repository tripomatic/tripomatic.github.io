## Change the language

1. Tap the **gear icon** on the home screen.
2. Tap **Language** and choose one of: English, Deutsch, Polski, Italiano, Português, Español, Nederlands, Français, Čeština.

The same setting changes the app's text and the narration. Tours are written in English and translated and voiced for the other languages. The first time a tour is played in a new language you may see **Preparing your narration** for a moment. If a language is not available for a tour, English plays instead.

Downloads are per language: after switching, download the tour again for offline use.

## Change distance units

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Units** and pick **Automatic**, **Metric (km, m)** or **Imperial (mi, ft)**.

**Automatic** follows your phone's measurement system. Units change the distances shown on screen. The spoken narration is fixed: English narration gives both metric and imperial, other languages use metric.
