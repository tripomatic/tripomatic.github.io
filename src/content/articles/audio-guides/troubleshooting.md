Work through these in order. Most problems are the first one: a weak GPS signal.

## The GPS signal is weak

Stops play automatically only when your location is accurate to better than about **50 metres** and you are inside the stop's zone. Tall buildings, narrow streets and being indoors make GPS less accurate, and it is often weak for the first minute after you start.

- Step outside or into a more open spot and wait a moment. The tour shows "Waiting for GPS" or "Weak signal" with the current accuracy.
- If the app thinks you may be at a stop but is not sure, it notifies you: "you may be nearby". Open the tour and play the stop yourself.
- You can always **tap the stop and press Play**.

## Location and permissions

- **Location access** must be allowed. **While Using the App** (iPhone) or **Only while using the app** (Android) is enough.
- **Precise location** must be on. With approximate location the app says it "isn't precise enough for stops to play automatically". On iPhone: Settings › Audio Guides › Location › Precise Location. On Android: Settings › Location › App permissions › Audio Guides › Use precise location.
- **Location services** must be on for the whole phone.
- **Always / Allow all the time** is optional. It only lets the app alert you near a stop while the app is fully closed.
- **Notifications** are optional. They only tell you when a stop could not play.

If something is missing, the tour shows a **You're ready to walk** checklist with **Turn on** or **Open Settings** buttons.

## The audio didn't load

If the tour was not downloaded and you lost your connection, you may see "you're here, but the audio didn't load". The app keeps retrying. To avoid it, [download the tour](/audio-guides/offline-downloads/) on Wi-Fi before you set out.

## The walk paused by itself

After a long time without activity the app pauses the walk so GPS is not left running. Open the tour and tap **Continue**.

## "New tours can't be loaded"

- "We can't confirm this copy of the app is genuine": update the app from the App Store or Google Play and check your phone's date and time are correct.
- "This network seems to block the services the app needs": try another Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Downloaded tours keep playing in both cases.

## Still not working?

In the app, tap the **gear icon** › **About & support** › **Contact support**. This opens an email to support@tripomatic.com with your app version and device already filled in, which helps us find the problem faster.
