Tripomatic Audio Guides turns a walk through a city into a narrated tour. You pick a tour, walk to the first stop, and the app plays each stop's story as you arrive. You can keep the phone in your pocket.

## What you get

- **Curated walking tours** in cities around the world, each with a route, walking times between stops and a map.
- **Automatic playback.** The app uses your phone's GPS to notice when you reach a stop and plays it, with a short chime first.
- **Offline listening.** Download a tour on Wi-Fi before you go and it plays without a connection, map included.
- **Nine narration languages**: English, German, Polish, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish, Dutch, French and Czech.
- **No account.** Nothing you do in the app requires signing in.

## Get the app

Search the App Store or Google Play for **Audio Guides by Tripomatic**, or use the links below. It needs iOS 16 or later on iPhone and iPad, or Android 8.0 or later. You can browse every tour on [tripomatic.com/en/audio-guides](https://tripomatic.com/en/audio-guides); tours play only in the app.

## How it relates to the Tripomatic planner

Audio Guides and the [Tripomatic trip planner](https://tripomatic.com/) are two separate apps from the same team:

| | Tripomatic | Audio Guides |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Plan multi-day trips on a map | Listen to guided walks on the spot |
| Account | Tripomatic account | None |
| Paid option | Premium subscription | One-time purchase per destination |
| Platforms | iPhone, Android, web browser | iPhone, Android |

A Tripomatic Premium subscription does not include audio guides, and an Audio Guides purchase does not unlock Tripomatic Premium.
