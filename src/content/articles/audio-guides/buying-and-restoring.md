## Buy a destination

1. Open a tour in the city you want.
2. Tap **Buy all guides**. The button shows the price in your currency, as set by the store.
3. Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your store password.
4. When you see **Destination unlocked**, every tour in that destination plays in full.

If you see **Purchase pending approval**, the store is waiting for something such as Ask to Buy approval from a family organiser. The destination unlocks on its own once it is approved. **This destination isn't available to buy yet** means the city is not on sale; its free stops still play.

## Restore purchases on a new phone or after reinstalling

Audio Guides has no account, so purchases come back through the store:

1. Make sure the phone is signed in to the **same Apple ID or Google account** you bought with.
2. In Audio Guides, tap the **gear icon** on the home screen to open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Restore purchases**.
4. You should see **Purchases restored**. **No purchases found to restore** means the store found nothing on this account: check you are signed in with the account you bought with.

Restoring brings back what you bought, not your downloads or tour progress. Downloads and progress are kept on each phone separately. Download the tours again from each tour's page; see [Download a tour for offline use](/audio-guides/offline-downloads/).
