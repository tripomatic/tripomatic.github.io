## Download a tour

1. Open the tour you want, ideally on Wi-Fi.
2. Tap **Download tour**. The size estimate is about 15–20 MB.
3. If your phone is low on space (under 100 MB free) or you are on mobile data, the app asks first. Tap **Download anyway** to go ahead.
4. Progress shows as a percentage. When it says **Downloaded**, you get a notification: "Ready offline". The whole tour can now play with no connection.

A download includes the narration, cover images, stop details and the offline map tiles for the route.

## Keep downloads up to date

If a tour is improved after you downloaded it, the button reads **Downloaded · update available**. Tap **Update** to get the new version. The old copy keeps playing offline until you do.

## Manage and delete downloads

1. Tap the **gear icon** on the home screen.
2. Tap **Downloaded tours**. It shows how much space downloads use.
3. For each tour, tap **Delete** to free space, or **Re-download**.

Downloads are kept for **3 months** and then removed automatically; the list shows "Auto-deletes in N days". Audio that the app cached while you streamed a tour, without downloading it, is cleared after about a week of not being used.

## Different language narration

Non-English narration is prepared the first time someone asks for it. If you see **Waiting for French audio** (or another language), leave the download running: it starts on its own when the narration is ready.
