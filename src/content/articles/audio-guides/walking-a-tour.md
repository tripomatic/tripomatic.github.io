## Start the walk

1. Open the tour and tap **Start tour** (or **Continue tour** if you started it before).
2. The first time, the app explains how it works: **Each stop plays as you arrive**. Tap **Start tour**.
3. Allow location access when asked. **While Using the App** is enough. See [Location and permissions](/audio-guides/troubleshooting/#location-and-permissions).
4. Allow notifications if you like. They are optional and only used when a stop cannot play.
5. Walk to the first stop. The map shows where you are and the route.

Headphones work best: the phone can stay in your pocket.

## While you walk

- **Arriving at a stop** plays it automatically, after a short chime and a vibration.
- **The screen can be locked.** Audio keeps playing and the lock screen has playback controls. On iPhone, the lock screen also shows the next stop, its distance and the walking time.
- **Tap any stop** in the list or on the map to load it, then press **Play**. Use the scrubber or skip 15 seconds back and forward.
- **Coming back to a stop** you half heard in the last hour resumes it; otherwise it starts again.
- The map follows you only if you ask: tap **Centre on my location**.

## Pause, continue, end

- **Pause** saves your progress: "You can resume anytime." Next time you open the tour the app asks **Continue your walk?**
- **End tour** finishes the walk and clears progress; starting again begins from stop 1.
- If the app sees no activity for an hour (or for 30 minutes when you are more than 1 km from the next stop), it pauses the walk itself and tells you. Your progress is saved.

Progress is kept on this phone only. It is not shared with your other devices.

## At the end

You see **Tour complete!** and can rate the tour from 1 to 5 stars. A written review is optional and may be shown publicly with your name.
