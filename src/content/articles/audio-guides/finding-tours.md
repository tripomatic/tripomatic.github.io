## Find a tour

1. Open **Audio Guides**.
2. Type a city into the search field on the home screen, or tap **Show tours near me**. The second option asks for your location.
3. Pick a destination, then a tour.
4. The tour page shows every stop, the route and the walking time between stops, for example "Walk ~6 min · 450 m".

You can also browse all tours on the web at [tripomatic.com/en/audio-guides](https://tripomatic.com/en/audio-guides) and scan the QR code there to open the same tour on your phone.

## What is free

Each tour card has a label:

- **Free tour**: every stop plays.
- **First 2 stops free** (or another number) / **First stop free**: those stops play; the rest are locked.
- **Intro free**: only the tour introduction plays until you buy.

The number of free stops is set per destination and can differ between cities. Locked stops show a lock icon and the message "Unlock this tour to listen". If you reach a locked stop while walking, the app tells you it is part of the full tour.

At the time of writing, the App Store and Google Play listings say **all tours are free for a limited time**. When a destination is sold, one purchase unlocks all of its tours. See [Buying a destination and restoring purchases](/audio-guides/buying-and-restoring/).
