First check where your subscription comes from: in Tripomatic, open **More › Settings › Tripomatic Premium** and tap **Subscription Information**.

## Bought on iPhone (App Store)

1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Apple Account › Subscriptions** (on older iOS versions, tap your name at the top, then **Subscriptions**).
3. Choose **Tripomatic** and tap **Cancel Subscription**.

## Bought on Android (Google Play)

1. Open the **Google Play** app.
2. Tap your profile picture › **Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions**.
3. Choose **Tripomatic** and tap **Cancel subscription**.

## Bought on the web

1. Sign in at [maps.tripomatic.com](https://maps.tripomatic.com/).
2. Open **More › Settings › Tripomatic Premium › Subscription Information**.
3. Tap **Manage in Customer Portal**. You can update your payment method or cancel there.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Old Sygic Travel subscriptions (FastSpring)

The **Subscription Information** screen offers **Manage in Customer Portal**, which opens the FastSpring account page. Sign in with your email there.

## Payment problems

If a payment fails, Settings shows **Payment issue** with a red **Fix now** button. Fix the payment method in the store or portal it points to.
