## Start the trial

1. Tap anything marked Premium, or open **More › Settings › Tripomatic Premium › Upgrade to premium**.
2. In **Upgrade to Premium**, tap **Start My Free Trial**.
3. Confirm with the App Store, Google Play or, on the web, the checkout form.

All Premium features unlock right away.

## Who gets a trial

- You are offered a trial unless your Tripomatic account started one in the **last 365 days**, on any device.
- On iPhone and Android the store has the final say: if your Apple ID or Google account has already used Tripomatic's trial, the store charges from day one. The button then says **Continue with Premium**.

## Cancel before you are charged

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid the first charge: see [Cancel or manage your subscription](/premium/cancel-subscription/). On iPhone and Android this is done in the store, not by deleting the app.
