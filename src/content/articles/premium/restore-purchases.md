## Usually: just sign in

Premium is tied to your **Tripomatic account**. Sign in with the same account (**More › Sign In / Create Account**) and Premium turns on, whichever device you bought it on.

## If Premium does not appear (iPhone or Android)

1. Make sure the phone is signed in to the **Apple ID or Google account you paid with**, and Tripomatic is signed in to your Tripomatic account.
2. Open the Premium screen: tap any Premium feature, or **More › Settings › Tripomatic Premium › Upgrade to premium**.
3. Under the purchase button, tap **Already purchased? Restore**.

What you may see:

- **Your purchase is pending approval**: the store is still processing it, for example waiting for Ask to Buy.
- **Your lifetime purchase was found. It is kept and applies when your subscription ends.**: you have both a lifetime licence and a subscription; nothing is lost.
- **No purchases found to restore**: see the questions below. If you used **Sygic Travel** before, tap **Get migration help** or follow [Bring over an old Sygic Travel account](/account/old-sygic-travel-account/).

Still missing? Email support@tripomatic.com with the receipt and your Tripomatic **User ID** (More › About › For Support Reference).
