## The two limits

1. **5 unlocked trip days per calendar month.** This is counted across the trips you own. When you go over it, the later days are **locked**.
2. **25 activities per trip.** Places and accommodation count, across every day and the shortlist. Flights, trains and other transport, free-time gaps and booked tours do not count. Items on locked days do not count either.

Shared trips (trips other people invited you to) never count and never lock.

## What a locked day looks like

A locked day shows a lock instead of its itinerary, and its places disappear from the map. Nothing is deleted. You cannot drag new items onto a locked day.

## How to unlock it

- **Wait for the next month.** The 5-day allowance renews every calendar month.
- **Start Premium.** All days unlock immediately and the 25-item limit goes away. New accounts can usually [try Premium free for 7 days](/premium/free-trial/).

The home-screen widget also respects this: on a locked day it shows **Unlock today's plan** instead of the plan.
