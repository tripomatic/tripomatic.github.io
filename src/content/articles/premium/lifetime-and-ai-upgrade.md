## What a lifetime licence includes

Settings shows it as **Purchased (classic)**. It covers:

- no ads, no free-plan day or item limits
- offline maps
- export to PDF, GPX and KML
- all map styles (Satellite, Outdoor, Winter)

## What needs the AI upgrade

These features belong to the subscription tier:

- the full AI assistant (beyond the preset questions) and higher AI day-plan limits
- live flight status and flight change pushes
- push notifications for dangerous situations on your trip
- uploading trip attachments

## Get the AI upgrade

1. Open **More › Settings › Tripomatic Premium**.
2. Tap **Upgrade**. The **AI Assistant Upgrade** screen opens ("Special offer for lifetime premium users").
3. Confirm in the App Store, Google Play or the web checkout.

The AI upgrade is a yearly subscription. If you cancel it, your lifetime licence stays.

## Lifetime licence from Sygic Travel

If you bought it under the Sygic Travel name and do not see it, link your old account first: [Bring over an old Sygic Travel account](/account/old-sygic-travel-account/). The licence comes across with the link.
