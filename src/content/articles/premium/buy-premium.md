## Buy in the app (iPhone or Android)

1. Sign in, or create a free account: **More › Sign In / Create Account**.
2. Open **More › Settings › Tripomatic Premium** and tap **Upgrade to premium**. Tapping any feature marked Premium opens the same screen.
3. Tap **Start My Free Trial** (or **Continue with Premium** if a trial is not available).
4. Confirm the payment in the App Store or Google Play.

## Buy on the web

1. Go to [maps.tripomatic.com](https://maps.tripomatic.com/) and sign in.
2. Open **More › Settings › Tripomatic Premium › Upgrade to premium**.
3. Complete the checkout. Payments on the web are handled by Paddle, Tripomatic's authorised reseller, and charged in your local currency.

## After buying

Premium is tied to your Tripomatic account. Sign in with the same account on any other phone or browser and Premium is active there too, no matter where you paid. Prices: [tripomatic.com/en/pricing](https://tripomatic.com/en/pricing).
