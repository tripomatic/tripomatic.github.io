## Rename the trip

1. In the **Trip** tab, tap the **⋮** (Trip menu) above the dates.
2. Tap **Trip Settings**.
3. Edit the name under **Trip Name & Dates**. It saves when you leave the field.

## Move the trip to other dates

In **Trip Settings**, tap **Change start date** and pick the new date. All days move together and keep their plans.

## Make the trip longer

- In the calendar, tap the date just before the first day or just after the last day, then tap **Extend '…'**.
- Or open a day's menu **⋮ › Rearrange days › Insert empty day before this one**.

Trips can be up to 82 days long.

## Make the trip shorter

Open the day you want to drop and choose **⋮ › Rearrange days › Remove this day**, then confirm. The day **and its places are removed**, and the following days move one date earlier. Move anything you want to keep to another day first (**Reschedule**). If you remove a day by mistake, restore it from [Version history](/planning/version-history/).
