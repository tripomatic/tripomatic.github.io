1. In the **Trip** tab, open a day's **⋮** menu and tap **Version history**.
2. Changes are listed newest first, for example "Added Louvre", "Moved … to another day" or "Removed a day", with who made them: a collaborator, **via AI assistant**, or a connected app.
3. Tap a change and choose **Go back to before this change**. This undoes that change **and all newer ones**. Confirm.
4. Changed your mind? Tap **Redo**.

Use **⋮ › Filter by day** in the history to see one day's changes. Older backups of a day can be brought back with **Restore this version**.

If restoring would re-add places beyond the [free plan limit](/premium/free-plan-limits/), the app asks you to upgrade.
