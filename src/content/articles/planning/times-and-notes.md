## Times, duration, cost and notes for a place

1. Tap the **Notes** icon (a document) on the item. **Edit Stop** opens.
2. Tap the title to **rename** the stop in this trip.
3. Tap **Set start/end times and duration** and use the **Start**, **Duration** and **End** tabs. If you set both start and end, the duration is calculated.
4. Tap **Set cost** to enter a price, or **Set as free**.
5. Tap **Add notes** to write notes (up to 1,800 characters; Markdown formatting such as **bold** and lists works).

Hotels open **Edit Accommodation**, transport **Edit Route**, tours **Edit Tour** and free-time blocks **Edit Free Time**, with the matching fields.

A place with a start time keeps its place in the order when you use **Optimize day** (Premium).

## Day title, notes and cost

1. Tap the day's title (or **⋮ › Edit day details**).
2. In **Edit day**, set a **day name** (up to 30 characters), the day's localities, **Day notes** and **Day cost**.

The **Day summary** at the end of the day adds up the costs as "Estimated cost", converting other currencies to yours. Change your currency in [Settings](/account/language-currency-units/).

## Free time

To keep a gap in the day, tap **Add to Day N › Free time** and set its duration.
