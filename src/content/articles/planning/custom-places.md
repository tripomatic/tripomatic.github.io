## From the map

- **iPhone and Android:** long-press an empty spot on the map.
- **Web:** click an empty spot on the map.

A card shows the address (or the town, if you are zoomed out). Tap **Add to trip** or add it to the shortlist.

## From inside a day

Tap **Add to Day N**, then:

- **Select from map**, and tap the spot;
- **Current location** (phones only); or
- type an **address** or **coordinates** (for example `50.0875, 14.4213`) into the search and pick **Use coordinates**.

Places you saved in **More › Frequent Locations** (home, work, a cottage) can be added the same way.

## Rename it and add details

Tap **Notes** on the new stop. In **Edit Stop**, tap the title to name it, and add times, cost and notes. It is labelled **Custom place**.

## Report a mistake on a public place

Open the place, tap **⋯ › Report an issue**, choose what is wrong (Permanently closed, Temporarily closed, Incorrect location, Incorrect contact info, Incorrect description, Wrong photos, Other), add details and tap **Submit report**.
