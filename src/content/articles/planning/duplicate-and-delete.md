## Duplicate a trip

1. Open the trip and tap the **⋮** Trip menu above the dates.
2. Tap **Duplicate Trip**, confirm, and choose a **start date** for the copy.
3. The copy opens, named "… (copy)", with all days, places, notes and costs. Sharing is not copied.

Duplicating is free. On the free plan, the copy's days count toward your [monthly days](/premium/free-plan-limits/) like any trip.

## Delete a trip

1. Open the trip, tap **⋮ › Trip Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone** and tap **Delete Trip**.
3. Confirm. The trip, its days and notes are removed permanently, also for anyone you shared it with.

## Leave a shared trip

On a trip someone else owns, **Trip Settings** shows **Leave Trip** instead. The trip disappears from your list and you lose access unless invited again.
