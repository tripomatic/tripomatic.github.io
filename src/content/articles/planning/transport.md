## Routes are added for you

When two places in a day are more than a few metres apart, Tripomatic adds a route between them: walking for short distances, car for longer ones and a flight for very long ones. Routes show the time and distance and are drawn on the map.

## Change how you get there

1. Tap the route between two places to expand it.
2. Choose **Walking**, **Car**, **Bike**, **Public transit**, **RV**, **Taxi**, **Scooter** or **Flight**.
3. For **Ferry**, **Bus**, **Train**, **Subway**, **Light rail**, **Monorail**, **Cable car**, **Funicular** or **Other**, tap **More transit types**.

Train, bus, ferry and the other manual types are not routed: the line is drawn straight and you enter the duration yourself in **Edit › Edit Route** (Departure, Duration, Arrival, cost and notes). Choosing one **locks** the leg so nothing is inserted between the two places; tap the lock to unlock it.

## Public transport

**Public transit** uses real timetables for the trip's date, with lines, platforms and departure times. If the timetable for your date is not published yet, you see an estimate based on the current one. Where no data exists, the app says "Public transport data is not available for this route"; choose another type.

## Add a flight

1. In the day, tap **Add to Day N › Flight** (you can also add one when creating the trip).
2. Search by **flight number**, for example LH400, or tap **Search by airports instead**.
3. Pick the flight and tap **Set flight**. The departure and arrival airports are added around it.

Not found? Tap **Enter flight manually** and type the airline and local times. Flight search covers about six months back and ahead.

Premium subscribers also get [live flight status](/on-the-road/live-flight-status/): delays, gates and baggage belts on the flight card, with pushes when something changes.

## Missing travel times

If you see **Travel times missing**, tap **Calculate travel times**.
