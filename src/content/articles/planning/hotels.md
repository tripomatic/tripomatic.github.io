Each day shows where you sleep at the start and end of the day. Empty nights show **Add accommodation**.

## Find and book a hotel

1. Tap **Add accommodation** on the evening of a day and choose **Find & book a hotel**. Or open **More › Hotels**.
2. The search uses your trip's nights and travellers. Tap **Edit stay** to change dates, adults, children or rooms, then **Update results**.
3. Filter by guest rating, stars, type, price, facilities or **With breakfast**, and sort by **Near your plan**, **Lowest price** and more.
4. Select a hotel and choose:
   - **Set as stay** to add it to your trip for the nights you pick (**Check all** / **Uncheck all**, then **Save**).
   - **Book** to open the partner's booking page with the best price found.

Hotel results come from **Stay22** and its partners. The price you set as a stay is filled in as the cost of the first night.

## Add a stay you already booked

1. Tap **Add accommodation** and choose **Add accommodation manually** ("Already booked, Airbnb, or an address").
2. Search for the hotel, campsite or address.
3. Tick the nights and tap **Save**.

## Change or remove nights

Tap **Set nights** on the stay and tick or untick nights. Unticking a night removes the stay from it.
