## Add a place

**From the map or search**

1. Tap a place on the map or find it with **Search**.
2. Tap **Add to trip** (the first button in the place's action bar).
3. In the **Add to trip** dialog, pick the day at the top. The place is previewed in the day with the routes recalculated. Tap a different gap to place it elsewhere.
4. Tap **Add here**.

If the place is already in your trip, the button reads **Add again** and offers to reschedule the existing copy.

**From inside a day**

1. Tap **Add to Day N** at the end of the day (or the day menu **⋮ › Add to this day**).
2. Search for places, hotels or addresses, or use **Select from map**, **Current location** (phones), **Flight** or **Free time**.

## Reorder and move between days

- **Drag and drop** an item to a new position. On iPhone and Android, **press and hold** briefly before dragging, so normal swipes still scroll.
- Drop an item on another date in the **calendar** to move it to that day, or on the **bookmark icon** to move it to the Shortlist.
- Without dragging: tap **Reschedule** on the item, choose a day and tap **Move to …** or **Copy to …**.
- Whole days: day menu **⋮ › Rearrange days** to swap with the previous or next day, insert an empty day, or remove the day.

## The Shortlist

The Shortlist holds places you want to visit but have not scheduled yet.

- Add: tap the **bookmark** icon on a place (**Add to shortlist**).
- Open: tap the bookmark icon next to the calendar in the **Trip** tab.
- Schedule: tap **Schedule** on a shortlisted place, or drag it to a day. With two or more places, **Plan days from your shortlist** lets AI pick the days for you; see [Plan this day](/ai/plan-this-day/).

## Remove a place

Open the item's **⋯** menu and tap **Remove from day** (or **Remove from Shortlist**). Removing a whole day asks for confirmation.

On the free plan a trip holds up to 25 places and stays; see [free plan limits](/premium/free-plan-limits/).
